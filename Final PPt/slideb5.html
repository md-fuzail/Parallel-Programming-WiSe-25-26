Slide 5: Architectural Comparison

Speaker : Fuzail
Approach A MPI_Allgatherv
The Hypothesis: The CPU cost of generating 64 million random numbers via complex bitwise shifts is too heavy.

The Technical Implementation: Divide the PRNG generation workload proportionally across all ranks (O(N²/P)). Once each node computes its slice, rely entirely on the high-speed cluster network (MPI_Allgatherv) to stitch the full 512 MB Matrix B together across all nodes.
Approach B Zero-Comm
The Hypothesis: Transmitting 512 MB across a shared, multi-tenant university cluster will cause massive network congestion and unpredictable latency.

The Technical Implementation: Completely bypass the network. Accept a redundant computational penalty where every single CPU independently generates the entirety of Matrix B locally (O(N²)). Achieve 0% network bandwidth consumption until the final verification gather.
