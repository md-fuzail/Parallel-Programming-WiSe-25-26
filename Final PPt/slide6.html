📈
Observation
ikj order keeps B rows in L1/L2 cache across the inner j-loop, greatly reducing cache misses vs. naive ijk.
⚠️
Limitation
Full B replica (N×N per node) limits scalability. For very large N, a Cannon's algorithm avoids this overhead.
